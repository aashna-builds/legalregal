# Hugel Executive AI Kit

AI toolkit for Hugel executives — legal document review, board support, industry research, executive correspondence, and investor relations prep.

Every skill in this kit follows the same rules: it figures out what it's looking at on its own rather than asking you to categorize it, it asks before producing anything (quick chat answer vs. a written document), it asks again before deciding how to deliver a document, and it asks clarifying questions only when something is genuinely ambiguous — never guesses on things that matter.

## Skills

### contract-review

Reviews contracts and agreements — distributor, HCP/practice, vendor, licensing, NDA, and other types — and flags risk areas using general pharma / medical device / aesthetics industry practice: territory & exclusivity, minimum purchase commitments, product liability & indemnification, IP/trademark licensing, regulatory compliance representations (FDA, off-label promotion, adverse event reporting), confidentiality scope, termination, and governing law.

### policy-review

Reviews internal company policies, SOPs, and procedures (HR, data privacy, quality/regulatory, code of conduct, EHS) for gaps, outdated regulatory references, unclear ownership, and internal inconsistency. Distinct from contract-review — this is for internal documents, not agreements between parties.

### board-deck-builder

Turns notes, a stated topic, or prior materials into a structured board-meeting presentation — clear narrative, one idea per slide, headline-style titles.

### board-memo-summarizer

Condenses long materials into a short board or executive pre-read: the ask, key highlights, risks, and a recommendation — nothing else.

### industry-research-digest

Pulls together a sourced research digest on regulatory changes, competitor moves, and market news relevant to medical aesthetics, pharma, and medical devices.

### executive-correspondence

Drafts executive-level correspondence — emails or notes to the board, investors, partners, or internal leadership — in the user's voice. Always shows the full final draft for review before anything is sent.

### meeting-prep

Builds a pre-meeting briefing: who's in the room, what's likely to come up, relevant history, and suggested talking points or questions.

### investor-relations-prep

Reviews or drafts investor-facing materials (earnings scripts, investor Q&A, presentations) for a publicly listed company — checking consistency with prior disclosures and flagging forward-looking-statement risk. Flags and drafts only; always recommends legal/IR compliance sign-off before anything goes external.
